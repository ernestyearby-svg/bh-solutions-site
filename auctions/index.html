How It Works

  1. Pick a vehicle from the listings.
  2. Submit pre-approval (required for bidding).
  3. Schedule inspection if available (optional).
  4. Place bid via the instructions we provide after approval.
  5. Winner gets confirmation + payment instructions.
  6. Pickup or delivery arranged within the agreed window.
